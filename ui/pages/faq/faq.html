QUESTIONS
Where is the wedding?

Our wedding will take place at Villa Nemora in Montebenichi, Bucine, Tuscany, nestled in the rolling hills of the Tuscan countryside between Florence, Siena, and Arezzo.

All events (welcome and farewell parties) will be held on the property at Villa Nemora.

How do I get to Villa Nemora?

The closest major airport is Amerigo Vespucci Airport (FLR) in Florence, approximately a 1-1.5 hour drive from Villa Nemora. Galileo

Galilei Airport (PSA) in Pisa is approximately 2 hours away, while Leonardo da Vinci International Airport (FCO) in Rome is about 2.5-3 hours away.

Do I need to rent a car?

We recommend renting a car if you plan to explore Tuscany before or after the wedding. Villa Nemora is located in the countryside, where public transportation and rideshare services are limited. If you don't plan to rent a car, we are happy to help coordinate a shared van from Florence to the venue!

Where should I stay?

We would love for you to stay with us at the venue! The property includes three villas all within an 8 minute walk. We will update this page with accommodation details and booking logistics soon!

Will the wedding take place indoors or outdoors?

All events will take place outdoors, barring inclement weather!

What should I wear?

Cocktail attire is preferred (tuxes and gowns welcome, as well as suits and cocktail dresses). Please be mindful of the fact that the majority of the wedding will take place on grass/gravel when choosing your shoes!

When should I RSVP by?

We'll set an official RSVP deadline when we send invitations. If you know you won't be able to attend, however, then we would greatly appreciate an early RSVP to help us plan!

Can I bring a plus-one?

Please refer to the RSVP page for information about plus-ones. If you have a plus-one, you will be prompted to respond on their behalf.

Are kids welcome?

While we love all your little ones, our wedding will be an adult-only event. We appreciate your understanding!

Do you have a registry or honeymoon fund?

Your presence at our wedding is truly the greatest gift we could ask for. We feel so grateful to celebrate this special day with the people we love, and we kindly ask that you don't feel any obligation to give us a gift.

If you would still like to contribute, we have created a honeymoon fund that can be found here.
